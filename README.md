**BCD Decade Counter PCB**

A digital electronics project implementing a BCD decade counter using a NE555 timer, 74LS90 decade counter, CD4511 BCD-to-7-segment decoder, and a common-cathode 7-segment display. The circuit was first prototyped and verified on a breadboard before being converted into a custom PCB using KiCad.

**Features**
1. NE555 astable oscillator as the clock source
2. 74LS90 decade counter (0–9)
3. CD4511 BCD-to-7-segment decoder
4. Common-cathode 7-segment display
5. Through-hole PCB design
6. External 5 V power input (MB102 breadboard power supply)
7. ERC and DRC verified

**Components**
1. NE555 Timer
2. 74LS90 Decade Counter
3. CD4511 BCD-to-7-Segment Decoder
4. Common Cathode 7-Segment Display
5. 7 × 220 Ω Resistors
6. 1 × 1 kΩ Resistor
7. 1 × 10 kΩ Resistor
8. 1 × 10 µF Capacitor
9. MB102 5 V Power Supply Module
